A NOTE ON THE SCIENCE

Evidence first. Imagination second.

Venom alone does not establish a snake’s shape, habitat or evolutionary history. This prototype chooses a broad body form from venom-similar references, blends compatible shapes, and uses a small model to adjust visual style. The model learned from authored illustrations, not photographs; individual peptide identities and study uploads are not available yet.
