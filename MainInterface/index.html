A NOTE ON THE SCIENCE

Evidence first. Imagination second.

Venom alone does not establish a snake’s shape, habitat or evolutionary history. This prototype compares reported categories and displays a separately authored species illustration. Individual peptide identities, study uploads and trained generation are not available yet.
