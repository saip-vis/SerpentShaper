A NOTE ON THE SCIENCE

Evidence first. Imagination second.

Venom alone does not establish a snake’s shape, habitat or evolutionary history. Reference-anatomy mode uses venom-similar illustrations and a style model; the shared-shape pilot blends three hand-authored references. Neither mode uses a photo-trained model. Individual peptide identities and study uploads are not available yet.
