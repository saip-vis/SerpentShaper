Early shape prototype. This is a simplified top-down illustration, not a species identification or an accurate anatomical reconstruction. The three control profiles are hand-authored design examples, not measurements. Venom sliders and ML are not connected to this lab yet.

What changes here?

Body and head proportions, hood width, markings, and the tail use the same controls for every reference. Their values move continuously as you change the blend.

Reference photography can inform future refinements. The photos and venom samples in this project are not known to be from the same animals.
