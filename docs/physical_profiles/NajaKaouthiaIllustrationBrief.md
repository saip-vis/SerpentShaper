# Naja kaouthia: illustration brief

Status: **brief drafted; healthy reference selection pending**. The previous SVG is a visual experiment and has not passed this brief. This document does not certify a scientific reconstruction.

## Files to give a designer or design agent

1. `config/illustration_specifications.json`: shared construction rules and species-specific view requirements.
2. `data/processed/morphology/physical_profiles.json`: find the exact `Naja kaouthia` record for sourced traits and evidence gaps.
3. Selected, identified, healthy reference photographs with their manifest provenance. These have **not** been selected yet. Do not treat every downloaded image as approved.

The structured brief joins to the catalog by `species_id`. Read the trait-specific source URL and locator rather than assuming one reference supports the whole animal. Missing numeric controls remain null. Any later artistic values must be stored separately and labelled as artistic choices.

## Deliverable

A coherent SVG plate with three labelled panels: a complete relaxed animal in lateral view; an expanded hood seen from behind; and an expanded hood seen from the front. Begin with an uncoiled full-body pose so that proportions and continuity can be inspected. A coiled display can follow after that anatomy works.

Build the body around a continuous centreline with smoothly varying width. The head, neck and tail must join without gaps or flat cut ends. The expanded hood is part of the neck, not a separate shield pasted onto a tube. Hidden edges must disappear correctly where anatomy overlaps. Draw the head from reference; do not use an oval as the final head design.

Use restrained shading and consistent outlines. Surface texture must follow local body curvature. A repeated diamond grid does not establish anatomical scale arrangement. Omit microscopic details until reference evidence supports them. Do not add a scale bar to a drawing with unmeasured proportions.

## Marking and view rules

The dorsal monocle is a single pale enclosure with a dark interior; its outline need not be a perfect circle. Do not replace it with the connected double-loop spectacle of the earlier cobra preset. Anchor it within the rear hood below the head. Ring size, thickness and position require a chosen reference, not an invented species constant. See [Wüster (1998), species account](https://www.academia.edu/1479078/W%C3%BCster_W_1998_The_cobras_of_the_genus_Naja_in_India_Hamadryad_23_15_32).

The front panel must use the ventral description, never mirror the dorsal monocle onto the throat. Maintain one consistent underlying anatomy across the views.

## Evidence and reference selection

The updated profile draws on the general diagnosis in [Tuong et al. (2021), pp. 114–116](https://journal.vnuf.edu.vn/en/article/download/503/428/842). Its examined specimen has a lost tail and was preserved. Its residual tail measurement and preservation color must not drive a healthy whole-animal illustration.

[Shi et al. (2022), section 3.3.2 and Figures 7–8](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9774835/) supply regional context and candidate living reference panels. Choose locality and life stage before selecting a palette. Historical Chinese records require taxonomic review following that paper's separation of N. fuxi. Do not blend the panels into an average animal. Some regional labels and banding statements in the text conflict; they are not numeric rendering defaults.

Local candidate pool: `data/image_pilots/pilot_108_naja_kaouthia/manifest.json`. Select clear full-body, rear-hood and throat views. Record the exact image identifiers, viewing direction, locality when known, attribution, license and selection rationale in the structured brief. Check identities and body condition for these selected references only; this does not require restarting bulk image review. Check figure-level photo permissions before copying or redistributing publication images.

## Acceptance before calling it a reference illustration

- Continuous healthy anatomy from snout to tail, without neck seams or detached shapes.
- Head outline, body taper and hood silhouette checked against the selected living references.
- Correct monocle surface and placement; front throat and back hood remain distinct.
- Reference provenance and uncertain features visible to the reviewer.
- Human visual review recorded. Passing these drawing checks alone does not establish expert anatomical approval.

This step improves the specification. It does not train a model, infer morphology from venom, approve downloaded photos, or replace the failed prototype with a finished illustration.
